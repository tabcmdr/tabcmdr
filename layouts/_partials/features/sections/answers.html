Instant answers

Math, time zones and conversions. The answer appears while you type.

Type a sum, a conversion or a city in the same search box. The result sits at the top of the list, and Enter copies it to your clipboard. No calculator tab, no search results page.

  • Math and units. Percentages, functions like sqrt(144), weights, distances, data sizes and CSS lengths.

  • Time and dates. The time in a city or time zone, date math like now + 3 days, and Unix timestamps.

  • Colors and number bases. Convert between HEX, RGB and HSL, or between decimal, hex, octal and binary.

  • Small developer tools. :tx changes text case, :uuid makes a UUID, :pwd makes a password.
