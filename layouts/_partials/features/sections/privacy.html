Privacy

Your tabs and history stay in your browser.

TabCmdr reads your tabs, bookmarks and history through the browser's own APIs, keeps them in memory while the palette is open, and drops them when it closes. None of it is sent to TabCmdr.

  • No account

    Install it and press the shortcut. There's nothing to sign up for and nothing to log in to.

  • No analytics or tracking

    The extension has no analytics, no telemetry, no tracking pixels and no cookies.

  • Browsing data isn't saved

    Tabs, bookmarks, history and downloads are read when you open the palette and kept in memory only. The icon cache keeps site names, not pages.

  • You choose the permissions

    Bookmarks, history, downloads and closed tabs are optional. Turn on only the ones you want, and turn them off any time.

  • AI goes straight to the provider

    Chats are sent from your browser to the provider you set up, with your own API key.

  • Settings sync through your browser

    Your theme, shortcut and other settings use the browser's own sync storage. TabCmdr never sees them.

Network requests happen only for features that need them: verifying a license with Gumroad, looking up the current price, weather from Open-Meteo, the RSS feeds you follow, the AI provider you chat with, and site icons. Read the privacy policy
