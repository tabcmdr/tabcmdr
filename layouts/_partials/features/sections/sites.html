Site shortcuts

Shortcuts for the sites you use all day. 208 of them, across nine sites.

On GitHub, Gmail, Figma and six other sites, the palette adds that site's own actions next to your tabs. Type issues on GitHub or compose in Gmail instead of learning each site's keys.

  • Search actions by name. Site shortcuts appear in the same results as your tabs and commands.

  • Turn sites on or off. Each site has its own switch in settings.
