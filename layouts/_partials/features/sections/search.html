:tTab search

Tab search across every window. Type a few letters, press Enter.

TabCmdr lists the open tabs from all your windows in one place. Type part of a title or an address and the matches rank as you type, even when the tab sits in another window.

  • Act on a tab without switching to it. Each row can reload, mute, pin, duplicate, open in incognito, move to a new window, copy its URL or close.

  • Pinned and closed tabs, too. :p lists pinned tabs and :r brings back tabs you closed.

  • Close as you go. ⌘⌫ closes the highlighted tab and keeps the list open.
