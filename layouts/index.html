search

Search everything

Tabs, pinned tabs, bookmarks, history, downloads, recently closed pages, URLs, and web search engines.

tab actions

Control messy tab bars

Pin, Mute, Reload, Duplicate, Move, Sort, Suspend inactive tabs, and Close duplicates.

ai chat

Bring your own models

Chat with configured providers, custom presets, model selection, history, streaming, and page context.

tools

Utilities inside the palette

Emoji picker, To-Dos, RSS feeds, Weather, Screenshot, QR codes, Color Picker, and Quick commands.

privacy

Local-first by default

The extension keeps browser data on your device, with settings and saved tool state stored locally.

keyboard

Keyboard-first flow

Open TabCmdr and quickly search tabs, pinned-tabs, history, recently closed, and keep moving without reaching for the mouse.

site shortcuts

Commands adapt to the page

GitHub, YouTube, Gmail, Notion, Figma, Discord, GitLab, Jira, and X actions appear when relevant.
