## Your computer's emoji panel

Windows and macOS each have an emoji panel that works in Chrome text boxes.

| To do this | Windows | Mac |
|---|---|---|
| Open the emoji panel | {{< kbd "Windows key" "." >}} while typing | {{< kbd "Fn/Globe" "E" >}}, or **Edit** > **Emoji & Symbols** |
| Find an emoji | Keep typing to search | Type a name in the search field |

Sources: Microsoft's [Windows keyboard tips and tricks](https://support.microsoft.com/en-us/windows/windows-keyboard-tips-and-tricks-588e0b72-0fff-6d3f-aeee-6e5116097942) and Apple's [Use emoji and symbols on Mac](https://support.apple.com/guide/mac-help/use-emoji-and-symbols-on-mac-mchlp1560/mac), checked in October 2026.

Both panels put the emoji straight into the text box you're typing in.

## Search and copy any emoji with TabCmdr {.way-tabcmdr}

TabCmdr is a keyboard launcher that lives in your browser. Its emoji picker opens over the page you're on, finds emoji by name or keyword and copies the one you pick, so you can paste it anywhere.

**Before you start:** add [TabCmdr from the Chrome Web Store](https://chromewebstore.google.com/detail/ejkkibnhfeccfnofeimpialoaipkojdc). It has a 7-day free trial and needs no account.

### Step 1: Open the emoji picker

Press {{< kbd "⌘" "K" >}} on a Mac or {{< kbd "Ctrl" "K" >}} on Windows and Linux, and type `:emoji`. The picker opens with every emoji grouped by category, such as **Smileys & Emotion**.

{{< screenshot src="emoji-picker-grid.png" alt="TabCmdr emoji picker showing the Smileys and Emotion grid with Grinning Face selected and skin tone dots" caption="`:emoji` opens the picker. The selected emoji and its name show at the top." >}}

For a shorter way in, type `:em` followed by a space. You can also open the **Tools** filter and choose **Emoji**.

### Step 2: Type a word

Type what you're looking for. Search matches emoji names and keywords, so `party` finds the partying face, party popper, balloon and more.

{{< screenshot src="emoji-picker-search-party.png" alt="TabCmdr emoji picker with party typed, showing Face With Party Horn And Party Hat and related emoji" caption="`party` finds emoji by name and by keyword." >}}

Use the arrow keys to move around the grid. {{< kbd "↑" >}} and {{< kbd "↓" >}} jump a row, {{< kbd "←" >}} and {{< kbd "→" >}} move one emoji.

### Step 3: Press Enter to copy

{{< kbd "Enter" >}} copies the selected emoji and closes the palette, and a message at the bottom of the page confirms it, such as **Copied 🥳**. Paste it with {{< kbd "⌘" "V" >}} or {{< kbd "Ctrl" "V" >}}. Clicking an emoji copies it too.

To copy the short name instead, press {{< kbd "Shift" "Enter" >}} or click **:name:**. The message then reads **Copied :partying_face:**.

## Pick a skin tone {.way-tabcmdr}

The six dots next to **Copy** set the skin tone, from the default yellow to five skin tones. Emoji that come in skin tones then show in the grid and copy in the tone you picked.
