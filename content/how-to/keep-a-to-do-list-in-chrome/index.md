## Add tasks from any page with TabCmdr {.way-tabcmdr}

TabCmdr is a keyboard launcher that lives in your browser. Its to-do list opens over the page you're on, so you can write a task down without opening a new tab or another app.

**Before you start:** add [TabCmdr from the Chrome Web Store](https://chromewebstore.google.com/detail/ejkkibnhfeccfnofeimpialoaipkojdc). It has a 7-day free trial and needs no account.

### Step 1: Open the to-do list

Press {{< kbd "⌘" "K" >}} on a Mac or {{< kbd "Ctrl" "K" >}} on Windows and Linux, and type `:todo`. The list opens, and the search box now reads **Add a todo here…**

### Step 2: Write the task

Type the task in the search box. Your open tasks are listed under **Todo** and finished ones under **Done**, with a count next to each.

{{< screenshot src="todo-list-typing-task.png" alt="TabCmdr to-do list with Call the dentist about Friday typed in the search box, two open tasks and two done tasks" caption="Type the task where you'd normally search." >}}

### Step 3: Press Enter

{{< kbd "Enter" >}} adds the task to the bottom of the **Todo** group and shows **Todo added**. The box clears, so you can add the next one straight away.

{{< screenshot src="todo-list-task-added.png" alt="TabCmdr to-do list with Call the dentist about Friday added as the third open task and a Todo added message" caption="The new task joins the list and the box is ready for the next one." >}}

## Tick off and clear tasks {.way-tabcmdr}

- **Done:** click the box next to a task. It moves to the **Done** group, crossed out. Click the box again to move it back.
- **Delete:** point at a task and click the **x** on the right. TabCmdr confirms with **Removed**.
- **Clear finished tasks:** click the **Clear** button under the **Done** group, such as **Clear 2 done**, to remove all of them at once.

Your tasks stay in TabCmdr's storage on this device, so they're still there after you close the browser. `:td` opens the same list.
