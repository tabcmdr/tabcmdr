## Overview

Accidentally closed a tab? TabCmdr can show you recently closed tabs and windows so you can restore them instantly.

## Enabling Recently Closed

This feature requires the optional **sessions** permission. To enable it:

1. Open the TabCmdr **Settings** page.
2. Find the **Recently Closed** (Sessions) toggle and turn it on.
3. Confirm the permission in the browser prompt.

## Restoring a Closed Tab

Once enabled:

1. Open the palette with **`⌘ K`** / **`Ctrl K`**.
2. Search for "recently closed" or type part of the page title.
3. Select the tab you want to restore.
4. Press **`Enter`** — the tab reopens exactly where you left it.

## Undo Close Tab

TabCmdr also exposes an **Undo close tab** command that restores the most recently closed tab without searching. Just open the palette, type "undo close", and press **`Enter`**.

## Privacy

Session data (titles, URLs, favicons of recently closed tabs) is loaded into memory when you open the palette and discarded when you close it. Nothing is stored or transmitted.
