## Overview

TabCmdr can search your browsing history so you can quickly revisit pages you've been to before — even if you don't remember the exact URL.

## Enabling History Search

History search requires an optional permission. To enable it:

1. Open the TabCmdr **Settings** page.
2. Find the **History** toggle and turn it on.
3. Confirm the permission in the browser prompt.

You can revoke this permission at any time.

## Searching History

Once enabled:

1. Open the palette with **`⌘ K`** / **`Ctrl K`**.
2. Type any part of a page title or URL.
3. History results appear in the results list.
4. Press **`Enter`** to navigate to that page.

## Timeframe Setting

You can control how far back TabCmdr looks when searching history. Adjust the **History timeframe** setting on the Settings page to limit results to the last day, week, month, or longer.

Narrowing the timeframe keeps results relevant and speeds up search.

## Privacy

History data is fetched from the browser API into memory when you open the palette and discarded when you close it. TabCmdr never stores, transmits, or modifies your history.
